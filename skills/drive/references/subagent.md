# As a Subagent

You were handed a brief instead of a topic, so you are its Subagent, whichever provider runs you. The rest of SKILL.md is the Orchestrator's.

1. Save a pasted brief verbatim to `.scratch/<topic>/<task>.brief.md` before starting; review agents read that file as the spec.
2. Reason by the implementer's Profile in [profiles.md](profiles.md).
3. Finish as [worktrees.md](worktrees.md) "How a Subagent finishes" says.
4. Hold your work against the Intent statement in the brief; record drift or a contradiction as the report's `Intent:` line, and do not interrupt the Operator for it.
5. Report in the format of [dispatch.md](dispatch.md).
